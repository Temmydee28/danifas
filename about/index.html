Our Culinary Story

Crafting Exceptional Dining Moments with Passion & Elegance

Founded with a vision to redefine catering and banquet services in Ondo State and across Nigeria, Danifas Catering and Event Service combines authentic African gastronomy with classical British silver-service techniques and contemporary continental cuisine.

Whether serving an intimate family celebration of 50 guests or managing high-volume state banquets and weddings of over 1,500 guests, our commitment to freshness, food safety, aesthetic presentation, and punctual service remains uncompromising.
