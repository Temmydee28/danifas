# Danifas Catering & Event Service

Modern, responsive website for **Danifas Catering and Event Service**, located in Alagbaka GRA Extension, Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria.

## Website Structure & Pages

- **Home (`index.html`)**: Hero carousel, feature services overview, video craft showcase, heritage story, specialties, testimonials, consultation banner, and unified footer.
- **About Us (`about/index.html`)**: Company history, culinary standards, executive leadership team, specialties, and gallery preview.
- **Services (`services/index.html`)**: Overview directory of all catering services and value propositions.
  - **Weddings (`services/weddings/index.html`)**: Bridal tastings, silver-service dining, champagne bars, and banquet coordination.
  - **Corporates (`services/corperates/index.html`)**: Executive luncheons, conference buffets, breakfast meetings, and corporate retainers.
  - **Private Parties (`services/parties/index.html`)**: Birthday feasts, anniversaries, penthouse dining, and live barbecue grills.
  - **Picnics (`services/picnics/index.html`)**: Outdoor grazing tables, gourmet lunch boxes, and fresh fruit coolers.
  - **Festivals (`services/festivals/index.html`)**: High-volume rapid-service food stations and concert catering.
  - **Social Events (`services/socials/index.html`)**: Naming ceremonies, family reunions, and traditional Nigerian feasts.
- **Menu (`menu/index.html`)**: Filterable interactive menu (Canapés, Main Courses, Grills, Sweets & Desserts, Signature Beverages).
- **Gallery (`gallery/index.html`)**: Filterable photo showcase with interactive lightbox modal.
- **Events & Packages (`events/index.html`)**: Silver, Gold, and Platinum catering packages, 4-step planning timeline, and FAQs.
- **Contact Us (`contact/index.html`)**: Comprehensive inquiry form, phone & WhatsApp direct links, location details, and operating hours.

## Technologies Used
- HTML5 & CSS3 (Custom CSS Grid & Flexbox)
- Bootstrap 5.3 & FontAwesome 6 (Vector Icons)
- Vanilla JavaScript (`js/main.js`) for navbar scroll effects, modal forms, menu filtering, and gallery lightbox.
